Move at agent speed, together. An agent can finish a task in minutes. What slows a team down is everything around the code: what's next, who has it, and whether it's really done. Tiller plans just far enough ahead, with each feature specified when its turn comes. `tiller next` ranks every legal move, the decisions waiting on you included. Claimed tasks run on their own branches, so people and agents work side by side without collisions.

Done means done, every time. Every piece of work knows what done means before anyone starts it. A feature's spec, acceptance criteria, and tasks are approved before the first line of code. Your own build, test, and eval commands are gates that block done, and Tiller records each result with the revision it ran against. Once a spec is approved it's pinned, so if it moves, Tiller flags the drift before work goes on.
